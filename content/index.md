# {{ title }}

The mezuzah goes on the left doorpost as you enter the house.


## One

First, say the blessing:

<div dir="rtl" align="right">

בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְיָ אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, אֲשֶׁר קִדְּשָׁנוּ בְּמִצְוֹתָיו וְצִוָּנוּ לִקְבֹּעַ מְזוּזָה

</div>

> Baruch atah Adonai eloheinu melech haolam asher kidshanu b'mitzvotav v'tzivanu likboa mezuzah

Blessed are You, Adonai our God, sovereign of space and time, who sanctified us with His commandments and commanded us to affix the mezuzah.

## Two

Put the mezuzah about two thirds of the way up the left doorpost, leaning toward the house interior.


## Three

After you've put up the mezuzah, say the blessing for doing new things:

<div dir="rtl" align="right">

בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה, יְיָ אֱלֹהֵינוּ, מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, שֶׁהֶחֱיָנוּ וְקִיְּמָנוּ וְהִגִּיעָנוּ לַזְּמַן הַזֶּה

</div>

> Baruch atah Adonai eloheinu melech haolam shehecheyanu v'kiyamanu v'higiyanu lazman hazeh

Blessed are You, Adonai our God, sovereign of space and time, who has kept us alive, sustained us, and brought us to this season.
